THE PROBLEM

You know your site isn't right. You just don't know where to start.

Your website gets traffic and people care about your cause, but donations plateau, sign-ups trickle, and everyone inside the organisation has a different theory why.

Most NFPs have a website. Far fewer have one that actually converts visitors into donors, supporters and participants — and reports back on how it's doing.

THE PRODUCT

Diagnosis before treatment.

The NFP Website Growth Audit is a structured review of your website through the eyes of the people you're trying to reach: donors, supporters, participants, partners. We look at twelve areas, grouped into four questions.

(01)

Can people find you?

SEO, analytics, and content. Are the right people arriving, from where, and what happens when they land?

SEOANALYTICSCONTENT
(02)

Do they get it in five seconds?

Messaging, brand, and content. Does the site say what you do, who it's for, and why it matters, in language supporters actually use?

MESSAGINGBRANDCLARITY
(03)

Can they get where they need to go?

UX, accessibility, mobile experience, and the donor and supporter journeys. We walk every critical path, on the devices your audience actually uses.

UXACCESSIBILITYMOBILE
(04)

Do they act, and can you tell?

Conversion, calls to action, and analytics. Where do people drop off, what's costing you donations, and is your measurement telling you the truth?

HOW IT WORKS

Three weeks. Four steps.

01

Kick-off (Week 1).

A 60-minute session on your goals, audiences and what "working" looks like. You give us access to analytics and any research you have.

02

The audit (Weeks 1–2).

We review all twelve areas, walk your donor and supporter journeys end to end, and dig into the data.

03

Roadmap (Week 3).

We turn findings into a prioritised plan: what to fix, in what order, and roughly what it takes.

04

The working session.

We present everything to your team, live. Questions encouraged.

This is for you if…

  • ✓You're an Australian NFP or for-purpose organisation with a website that matters to your mission.
  • ✓You suspect your website is underperforming but can't pinpoint why, or your team can't agree on why.
  • ✓You're weighing up a redesign and want evidence before you commit real budget.
  • ✓You have (or can get) access to your own analytics and website.

It's not for you if…

  • ✕You've already decided exactly what to build and just need hands on keyboards. (Talk to us about a website project instead.)
  • ✕Your website is brand new and hasn't had time to gather real data.
  • ✕You want a report that validates a decision you've already made. We'll tell you what we find, even when it's awkward.
