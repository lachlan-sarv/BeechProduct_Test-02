OUR APPROACH

How we do it

Five steps, two weeks, zero fluff.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We start with a conversation: your goals, your audience, and where you think the site is letting you down.

  2. 02

    Full-site walkthrough

    We move through every page like a first-time visitor and a returning donor, flagging friction as we go.

  3. 03

    Data & behaviour analysis

    Analytics, heatmaps, funnels and drop-off points tell us what people actually do — not what we assume they do.

  4. 04

    Benchmarking

    We compare your site against peer and aspirational organisations to see where you're ahead, and where you're behind.

  5. 05

    Findings & roadmap

    Everything lands in one prioritised report: what to fix first, what can wait, and what to leave alone.
